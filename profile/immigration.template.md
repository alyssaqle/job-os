# Work Authorization

> Copy this file to `immigration.md` and fill in your status. Your `immigration.md` is gitignored. Keep it neutral and factual.

A generic, neutral place to record your work-authorization status and a short positioning statement you can reuse in applications. No specific visa type is baked in; fill in what applies to you.

## Status

- **Authorized to work in:** <country/region>
- **Requires sponsorship:** <yes / no>  (keep this consistent with `requiresSponsorship` in your profile)
- **Current basis (optional):** <e.g. citizen, permanent resident, work permit, employer-sponsored>
- **Notes (optional):** anything an employer would reasonably need to know, stated plainly.

## Positioning statement

A short, professional line for "are you authorized to work" or comments fields. Keep it calm and factual. Pick or adapt one:

> Example (no sponsorship needed): I am authorized to work in <country> and do not require sponsorship.

> Example (sponsorship needed): I am authorized to work in <country> and would require sponsorship for continued employment. I am happy to share details on request.

## Guidance for the assistant

- Use only what is written here. Do not infer or invent visa specifics.
- Keep wording neutral and professional. Avoid speculation about timelines or legal outcomes.
- Mirror the employer's own field labels when filling forms.
