# Application Style

> Copy this file to `application-style.md` and fill in your angles. Your `application-style.md` is gitignored.

How to write free-text application answers (cover-letter fields, "why this company", "describe a project").

## Principles

- **Crisp.** Answer the question asked, then stop. No padding.
- **Grounded in real work.** Pull specifics from `experience.md`. If you cannot back a claim, do not make it.
- **Specific to the company.** Reference the actual product, team, or problem. Generic answers read as mass-applied.
- **First person, plain voice.** Sound like you, not a press release.
- **No em dashes.** Use commas or "and". They signal AI-generated text.
- Match length to the field. A 300-character box is not an essay.

## A reusable shape for "why you / why us"

1. One line on what specifically drew you to this role or product.
2. One concrete example from your experience that maps to their need.
3. One line on what you would do or care about in the role.

## Company-specific angles

Add a short block per target company so answers stay sharp. Example:

### Acme Corp

- **Why them:** Their work on <product/problem> lines up with my background in <domain>.
- **Best matching example:** <project from experience.md> shows I can <do the thing they need>.
- **Watch-outs:** <anything to address proactively, e.g. a gap to frame well>.

> Add one block per company you are seriously pursuing.
